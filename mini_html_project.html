VIRAT KOHLI

A photograph of Virat Kohli

Biography

Virat Kohli was born on November 5, 1988, in Delhi. He showed a deep love for cricket at age three. At nine, he joined the West Delhi Cricket Academy. A right-handed batsman and brilliant fielder, he debuted in Tests in 2011. He became India's captain in 2014.

Achievements

  • 2008 - Made his senior international ODI debut for India against Sri Lanka.
  • 2011 - Won the ICC Cricket World Cup with the Indian team, scoring a century in his debut World Cup match.
  • 2012 - Won the prestigious ICC ODI Cricketer of the Year award.
  • 2013 - Honored with the Arjuna Award for achievements in sports.
  • 2014 - Named the permanent Test captain of the Indian cricket team.
  • 2016 - Scored a record-shattering 973 runs in a single Indian Premier League (IPL) season with 4 centuries.
  • 2017 - Conferred the Padma Shri, India's fourth-highest civilian honor.
  • 2018 - Won the Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna (now Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna), India's highest sporting honor.
  • 2020 - Named the ICC Male Cricketer of the Decade (2011–2020) and ICC ODI Cricketer of the Decade
  • 2024 - Won the ICC Men's T20 World Cup with India and announced his retirement from T20 internationals as a champion.
